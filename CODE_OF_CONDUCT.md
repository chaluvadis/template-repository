# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this project a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful space for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or perspective.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, and participants to help create a positive environment.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community:

- Being respectful and considerate in all interactions
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without permission
- Disruptive or disrespectful conduct
- Any behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Public and private communications related to the project
- Any events or communities associated with the project

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with these guidelines.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting:

**Email:** conduct@example.com

All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and fairly.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps depending on severity:

1. **Warning** – A private written warning explaining the issue
2. **Temporary Ban** – Suspension from project participation
3. **Permanent Ban** – Removal from all project spaces
